# Incident communications and escalation

| Audience | Owner | Trigger / route | Update cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incident team | IC | SEV-1 bridge and out-of-band fallback | 15 minutes |
| Executives / BCRB | IC | objective breach, integrity issue, material disruption | 30 minutes or material change |
| Merchants | Communications + Payments | confirmed impact and safe retry guidance | approved status cadence |
| Sponsor bank / NPCI | Payments + Legal | contractual/participant event threshold | contract-defined |
| RBI / CERT-In / law enforcement | Compliance + Legal + Security | applicable statutory trigger and deadline | law and direction driven |
| PCI assessor / acquirer | Compliance | incident impacting CDE or contractual trigger | contract / response plan |

Record recipient, channel, authorizer, sent time, facts, next update. Do not publish speculative cause, sensitive security details, PAN or credentials. For suspected identity compromise use out-of-band validated contacts; never include secret material in bridge notes.
